Before you begin

What will I be asked to do?

Read individual pull-request descriptions, judge their likely authorship, and give a short reason. Please make independent judgments and don’t search for the original pull requests.

Is the study open yet?

Not yet. This demo uses three fictional, AI-created examples to test the interface, not measure authorship accuracy. Participation and consent details will be added before the real study opens.

Does this preview save anything?

Demo answers stay in this tab’s memory until you reload or close it. You can download a labeled demo JSON file at the end. Nothing is submitted; there are no analytics, cookies, or browser storage. Hosting providers may separately keep access logs.
